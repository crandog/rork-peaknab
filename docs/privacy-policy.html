Privacy Policy

PeakNab LLC
Effective Date: July 12, 2026

Overview

PeakNab ("we," "us," or "our") operates the PeakNab mobile application (the "App"), a summit-tracking tool that lets you record mountain ascents, view peaks on an interactive map, and export your climbing log. This Privacy Policy describes what data we collect, how we use it, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

By using PeakNab, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, you should not use the App.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect the following:

  • Email address — used for authentication and account identification.
  • Name — collected only if you sign in with Apple and choose to share it. Email/password and Google sign-in do not transmit your name to us.
  • User ID — a unique identifier generated by our authentication provider (Supabase) to associate your data with your account.

Summit Records

When you log a summit, the App stores the following data:

  • Mountain identifier and summit date
  • Free-text summit report and conditions notes that you write
  • A photo URI referencing a local image on your device (the image file itself is not uploaded to our servers; only the local file path is stored in your record)
  • Timestamp of when the record was created

Custom Mountains

If you add a custom mountain that is not in the built-in catalog, the App stores the details you enter (name, elevation, coordinates, description).

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To store and sync your summit records across devices
  • To display your personal climbing log and statistics
  • To generate a PDF export of your summit history (entirely on-device)
  • To share your summit achievements via the native iOS share sheet (only when you choose to share)

We do not use your data for advertising, tracking, profiling, or sale to third parties. PeakNab contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics trackers, and does not participate in any ad network or data brokerage.

Where Your Data Is Stored

On Your Device

All summit records and custom mountains are stored locally on your device first, using the device's built-in key-value storage. This means your data is available even when you are offline, and the App functions fully without an account.

In the Cloud (When You Sign In)

When you create an account or sign in, your summit records and custom mountains are synced to a secure cloud database so you can access them across devices and recover them if you switch phones. If you use the App without signing in (demo mode), your data stays entirely on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Third-Party Services

PeakNab relies on the following third-party services. Each operates under its own privacy policy. We do not share your data with any other third parties.

Apple (Sign in with Apple) Apple ID token, name (if you choose to share) Authentication on iOS

No analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs are included in PeakNab. We do not use Firebase, Sentry, Amplitude, Mixpanel, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, or any similar tracking service.

Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account (see below), all of your data is permanently removed from our servers.

If you use the App without an account, your data exists only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App or clear the App's local data.

Deleting Your Account and Data

You can delete your account at any time directly within the App:

  • Open the Profile tab
  • Scroll to the Danger Zone section
  • Tap Delete Account
  • Confirm the two-step deletion prompt

When you delete your account:

  • Your Supabase authentication user is permanently deleted
  • Your user_data row (containing all summit records and custom mountains) is deleted from the cloud database
  • All local data on your device (summit records, custom mountains, session tokens) is erased
  • This action cannot be undone

If you are unable to access the App, you may also request account deletion by emailing hello@peaknab.com.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do not share your data with third parties except as described in this policy (authentication providers and map tile delivery). We will only disclose your data if required to do so by law.

Tracking and Advertising

PeakNab does not track you across other apps or websites. The App does not use the App Tracking Transparency framework, does not access the IDFA, and does not include any advertising or attribution SDKs. You will not be asked to allow tracking because the App does not engage in any tracking activity.

Children's Privacy

PeakNab is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Your Privacy Choices

  • Use without an account: You can use PeakNab in demo mode without creating an account. Your data stays on your device only.
  • Sign out: Signing out stops cloud sync. Your local data remains on your device.
  • Delete your account: Permanently erases all your data from our servers and your device.
  • Photo library: The App requests access to your photo library only when you choose to attach a photo to a summit report. You can deny this permission and the App will function normally without photos.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
